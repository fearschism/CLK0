01

Overview

The TGS Saudi website is a bilingual (English / Arabic) single-page marketing site for the Saudi member firm of the TGS global network. It presents the firm’s expertise, clients, story, and contact channels with a modern, confident, and distinctly branded experience.

Design principles

Confident & professional

A clean, editorial layout with generous whitespace communicates trust and expertise for a middle-market advisory audience.

Distinctly branded

The TGS orange is used deliberately as an accent and energy colour against a light, neutral canvas.

Quietly modern

Subtle motion — an interactive hero field, sliding carousels, reveal-on-scroll — signals a forward-looking firm without distraction.

Bilingual by design

Every screen is built to mirror cleanly between left-to-right English and right-to-left Arabic.

Brand Identity

Logo

The mark pairs the global TGS glyph — set in white inside a rounded orange tile — with the word Saudi in brand orange. The mark and wordmark are locked together and always read left-to-right, even in Arabic layouts (only the whole lockup mirrors position). The tile also serves as the favicon.

04

Components

Navigation

Sticky header

Logo, primary links, EN/AR toggle, and a primary “Contact us” button. Compacts on scroll; collapses to a menu panel on mobile.

Actions

Buttons

Primary (solid orange) and outline (orange border) styles, with a soft hover lift and colour deepening.

Content

Cards

Pillar, expertise, client, insight, and team cards share a white surface, hairline border, rounded corners, and a lift-on-hover.

Motion

Carousel / marquee

Expertise cards and client logos scroll horizontally in a seamless loop, pausing on hover and reversing direction in RTL.

Forms

Contact form

Name, company, service selector, and message. Submits via a pre-filled mailto draft to info@tgs-saudi.com.

Location

Map

Embedded interactive map pinned to the Riyadh office (Al Sahafah District) with an overlaid “Our office” card and maps link.

Identity

Footer

An immersive white-to-orange gradient with soft orange glows, holding navigation, contact details, and legal links.

Social proof

Client marquee

A continuously scrolling band of client chips under the hero to establish trust “across the Kingdom”.

Motion & Interaction

Motion is purposeful and brand-coloured — it adds life and a sense of modernity while keeping content legible.

InteractionBehaviour
Hero dot fieldA canvas grid of orange dots repels away from the cursor and springs back, creating a live ripple across the hero whitespace.
Heading revealHovering “TGS Saudi Arabia” transitions it to orange with a glowing drop-shadow and a light beam sweeping across the letters.
Expertise carouselContinuous horizontal auto-scroll (~28s loop), paused on hover, direction-aware for RTL.
Client marqueeSlower continuous scroll (~38s) with edge fade masks.
Reveal on scrollSections and cards fade and rise into view as they enter the viewport.
Ambient blobsSoft organic shapes drift gently behind the hero and footer.
07

Internationalisation

The site ships in English and Arabic with a single toggle, and is fully mirrored for right-to-left reading.

  • All copy is stored as paired EN / AR strings; toggling swaps language instantly with no reload.
  • Switching to Arabic sets dir=rtl, mirroring layout, alignment, and marquee direction.
  • The logo lockup stays left-to-right in both languages; only its position mirrors.
  • Numerals and the office map remain consistent across languages.

Accessibility & Content Notes

  • Colour pairings target legible contrast; orange is reserved for accents and large text, not long body copy.
  • Decorative canvas and blobs are non-interactive (aria-hidden) and never block clicks or content.
  • Navigation, forms, and buttons are keyboard operable with visible focus.
  • Client names and team profiles are placeholders for demonstration and should be replaced with approved content before launch.
  • Imagery should carry meaningful alternative text once final assets are supplied.
